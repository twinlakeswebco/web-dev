Where to Hire a Web Designer: Comparing Your Options

Compare referrals, local studios, independent professionals, agencies, and freelance marketplaces by relevant work, accountability, scope, and.

Published November 11, 2026 · Casey Keown

A marketplace profile, local recommendation, or agency website can all look convincing. Each is a starting point for a conversation, not proof that the fit is right.

The direct answer

Compare referrals, local studios, independent professionals, agencies, and freelance marketplaces by relevant work, accountability, scope, and ownership. The hiring channel does not replace vetting.

Referrals and local providers

Ask business owners with useful websites about the working relationship, not only the design. Find out who handled content, how updates work, and whether they received their accounts.

A local Kentucky provider may be able to meet, photograph the business, and understand the service area quickly. Confirm whether those tasks are actually included. A nearby mailing address does not make a vague agreement clearer.

Independent professionals and agencies

An independent professional can offer direct communication and a compact process. Ask about capacity, backup arrangements, specialist help, and support when they are unavailable.

An agency may provide several specialties, but ask who will do the work and who can make decisions. A larger team is useful when the project needs those skills. It can also add coordination that a small brochure site does not require.

Freelance marketplaces

Marketplaces can help you discover candidates and compare defined services. Read the platform’s current terms for fees, payment protection, dispute procedures, communication, and ownership before using it.

Do not assume a platform badge verifies every skill. Request a walkthrough of relevant work, define the deliverable, and use milestones. Keep important project details in the agreed written record.

Two places to start a shortlist

Fiverr’s website-design category is one place to compare defined freelance offerings. Read the scope carefully to distinguish a design file from a fully built and launched website. Clutch’s Kentucky web-design directory offers another discovery route for companies. Clutch discloses that it may earn fees for some placements, so do not treat display order as independent proof of the best fit.

These are discovery resources, not my endorsements of every listed provider. Verify the actual person, relevant work, current terms, and handoff before committing. You can also speak directly with a local independent provider, including Twin Lakes Web Co., without using a marketplace.

Send the same brief to every candidate

Use the same service description, page list, functions, content responsibilities, deadline, and handoff expectations. That makes differences in proposals easier to interpret.

Avoid asking several people for free finished designs. A portfolio and process discussion should establish fit. If detailed discovery is needed, agree on a paid, bounded deliverable.

Use one brief across hiring channels

Send the same description to a local studio, an independent professional, and a marketplace candidate. Include the customer action, content you have, required integrations, date, and handoff. If one candidate asks useful questions, add the clarified answer to the brief for everyone. You are comparing their proposed work and judgment. You are not comparing how well each person guessed the missing information.

Before you decide

  • Shortlist by relevant work.
  • Compare the same written brief.
  • Confirm the actual builder and contact person.
  • Read payment and handoff terms.

Which hiring platform is best?

There is no universal winner. Choose a hiring route that gives you relevant candidates and clear accountability for your particular project.
