Grayson County's Most Profitable Nonprofit Made Nearly $49 Million. Leitchfield Is Still Being Sent Down the Road.

Nonprofit Owensboro Health Twin Lakes has posted nearly $49 million in surpluses since 2021 while staffing fell and care gaps sent Grayson County patients elsewhere.

Published November 22, 2026 · Casey Keown

Owensboro Health Twin Lakes Medical Center is a tax-exempt charity. Since 2021 it has reported $13 million to $15 million a year more in revenue than expenses. Its yearly surplus alone is larger than the entire budget of any other nonprofit based in Grayson County. Over the same years, payroll staffing and inpatient volume fell, spending on outside contractors rose, and a federal inspection documented a patient turned away at the door. Current and former employees describe what that looks like from the inside.
