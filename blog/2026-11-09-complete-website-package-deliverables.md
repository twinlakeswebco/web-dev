# What a Complete Website Package Should Deliver

You can buy a complete layout and still need to supply every word, photograph, policy, and account. Clarify the deliverables before comparing package names.

## The direct answer

A complete package should name its pages, content work, functions, testing, launch tasks, ownership, and support. The label complete has no useful meaning without that list.

## Separate pages from capabilities

A services page is a page. Quote uploads, appointment requests, inventory, payments, and staff editing are capabilities. List both. A small page count can still involve a complex workflow.

Ask who writes the copy, checks service facts, prepares images, and configures integrations. If you supply content, ask for the format and deadline. A proposal should explain how incomplete content affects the schedule.

## Name the technical baseline

Discuss HTTPS, crawlable links, descriptive titles, canonical URLs, a sitemap, mobile layouts, accessibility checks, and form delivery. “SEO included” should identify actual tasks and avoid promising rankings.

[Google’s SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) describes useful content and technical fundamentals. It is a better reference for deliverables than an unexplained promise to “optimize everything.”

## Define the handoff

You should know where the domain, hosting, CMS, analytics, and form accounts live. Ask for training or written editing instructions if you will make updates. Confirm whether the source can be moved and what paid licenses remain necessary.

If the package includes ongoing maintenance, name response expectations and the work covered. Hosting availability, fixing defects, and adding new features are separate responsibilities.

## Check the launch responsibilities

Who connects the domain, checks important redirects, verifies forms, tests share previews, and confirms indexing settings? Who handles business email records during a DNS change?

For a Grayson County business, the package may also include service-area copy or photography coordination. Those should be explicit items, not assumptions based on a local provider’s address.

## A package comparison example

One package might include owner-supplied copy, a standard page structure, and a simple request form. Another might include original writing, content organization, project photography preparation, and a booking integration. Both can be good purchases. The useful comparison identifies what you must do yourself and which subscriptions remain. Ask the provider to show a sample handoff so training and account delivery have a concrete meaning.

## Before you decide

- Pages and customer actions are listed.
- Content ownership is assigned.
- Recurring charges are disclosed.
- Launch and handoff are defined.

## Does complete mean unlimited revisions?

No. Revisions and additional work should have their own clear terms.
