# After Website Launch: Divide Support Responsibilities Clearly

A site can launch successfully and still become hard to operate if everyone assumes someone else is handling renewals and updates.

## The direct answer

After launch, assign responsibility for hosting, updates, backups, content changes, third-party services, and defects. A support agreement should say what is covered and how to request help.

## Separate the types of support

Hosting keeps the site available. Maintenance addresses software and operational upkeep. Content support changes business information. New development adds capabilities. A single monthly fee may cover some of these and exclude others.

Ask for examples. Does changing hours count? What about adding a page, connecting a new booking system, or restoring a deleted image? Specific examples prevent vague expectations.

## Plan recovery

Know where backups live, what they contain, how often they run, and who can restore them. An export that has never been restored is incomplete evidence of recovery readiness.

Also document account recovery. Domain, hosting, CMS, forms, and analytics should not depend on a password saved only on an outside vendor’s laptop.

## Review third-party dependencies

Forms, booking, payments, email, and embedded tools can change independently of the website. Record who receives renewal and outage notices.

A Kentucky business using a booking service needs someone to review hours and staff availability. The developer cannot infer changes that the business never communicates.

## Create a routine

Check contact details and form delivery monthly. Review service information, photos, policies, and software responsibilities on an agreed schedule. Keep a small record of changes.

If support ends, request a handoff before canceling accounts. Preserve the domain and business email while the next provider prepares the transition.

## Practice a content update before handoff

Ask the provider to watch you make one ordinary update using your own account. Change a draft service paragraph or replace a noncritical image, then restore it. You will learn whether the instructions are usable and whether your permissions are appropriate. If updates are managed by the provider, practice submitting a request and confirm the expected turnaround and price instead.

## Before you decide

- Name each responsibility.
- Document recovery.
- Define response expectations.
- Keep renewal contacts current.

## Should every site have a monthly maintenance plan?

Every site needs assigned upkeep. Whether that requires a paid monthly plan depends on the platform, features, and owner’s capacity.
