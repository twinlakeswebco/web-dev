# Why Your New Website Is Not Getting Traffic

A new website can work perfectly and still receive few visitors. Publishing starts the process; it does not create demand by itself.

## The direct answer

First check whether pages are discoverable and indexed, then whether they target useful customer searches. Separate technical access, search demand, competition, and conversion before choosing a fix.

## Check technical access

Open the live pages, confirm they return normally, inspect noindex and robots settings, and verify the intended canonical URLs. Look for a sitemap and crawlable internal links.

Use Search Console to inspect representative pages. A page can be discovered without being indexed, or indexed without receiving impressions. Those are different problems.

## Review what the pages answer

Does each page explain a real service or buyer question? Does it state genuine Kentucky coverage and a clear next step? Vague copy can leave both visitors and search engines unsure what the page is for.

Avoid publishing dozens of nearly identical town pages. Improve the substantive service content and use location information where it helps the reader.

## Understand impressions and clicks

No impressions may point toward discovery, indexing, relevance, or competition. Impressions without clicks suggest reviewing query fit, titles, descriptions, and the results around you.

Clicks without inquiries require a separate conversion review. Check calls, form delivery, trust, pricing clarity, and service fit before buying more traffic.

## Improve one layer at a time

Fix technical blockers first. Then improve the service pages and internal links. Maintain accurate business listings and seek relevant mentions through real relationships and useful work.

[Google’s SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) explains the fundamentals. [Its AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) does not prescribe a shortcut that guarantees AI citations. Clear answers and accessible pages support discovery without guaranteeing placement.

## Separate visibility from lead quality

If Search Console shows impressions for unrelated searches, improve page focus. If useful searches produce clicks but no inquiries, review trust and contact paths. If the office receives inquiries that never become quotes, investigate fit and follow-up. Each layer suggests a different next step. Rebuilding the entire site without diagnosing the layer can spend money while leaving the actual problem untouched.

## Before you decide

- Verify indexability.
- Inspect Search Console.
- Review query and page fit.
- Test conversion separately.

## How long should I wait before changing everything?

Use evidence rather than a fixed waiting period. Fix confirmed technical failures immediately, and evaluate content changes with enough data to understand the effect.
