# Paying a Web Designer: Deposits and Milestones

A payment schedule should make it clear what happens next for both the owner and the builder.

## The direct answer

Match payments to a defined scope and reviewed milestones. Confirm deposits, recurring charges, cancellation terms, ownership transfer, and final handoff before paying.

## Understand what the deposit buys

A deposit can reserve capacity and fund initial work. Ask whether it is refundable, what work begins, and what happens if the project is delayed or canceled.

Small fixed services may be prepaid. Larger uncertain projects often benefit from staged commitments. There is no universal percentage that makes every agreement safe.

## Tie milestones to outputs

Examples include an approved page plan, accepted design direction, functioning staging build, and completed launch checks. Avoid milestones defined only as an unexplained passage of time.

Decide how long you have to review and what constitutes acceptance. A milestone should be specific enough that both parties know whether it is complete.

## Keep changes separate

Approve added cost before new scope begins. Record the requirement, price, and schedule effect. Do not let informal requests accumulate into an unexpected final invoice.

Also separate third-party subscriptions. Know which services charge you directly and which are included in a managed fee.

## Verify final handoff

Before final acceptance, review agreed customer tasks, account access, files, documentation, and support. Follow the contract’s payment and transfer terms.

For a disputed or high-value agreement, seek qualified advice about your circumstances. Practical project planning does not replace legal review.

## Keep the invoice tied to the agreement

Before paying a milestone invoice, compare it with the agreed output and review process. Ask about discrepancies promptly and keep the written response. Do not add payment conditions after the work is delivered that were never part of the agreement. A fair process protects the builder’s completed work and the owner’s ability to verify what was purchased.

## Before you decide

- Read deposit terms.
- Define outputs.
- Approve changes in writing.
- Confirm handoff and recurring bills.

## Should I withhold all payment until launch?

That may not fit the provider’s business model or your agreement. Negotiate a clear staged plan before work starts.
