# What SEO Should Be Included in a New Website Build?

“SEO included” can describe anything from a title field to months of content work. Ask for the actual tasks.

## The direct answer

A new build should include crawlable pages, useful service content, descriptive titles, canonical URLs, a sitemap, sensible internal links, and indexing checks. Ongoing growth work is a separate scope.

## Build the foundation during development

Organize services around customer questions, use readable URLs, and ensure important text is available to search engines. Protect useful old URLs with relevant redirects during a redesign.

Check that production pages are not accidentally marked noindex. Provide an XML sitemap and verify the domain’s Search Console ownership. Submission helps discovery; it does not guarantee indexing.

## Write for a real Kentucky audience

Explain actual services and coverage. A statewide provider can describe statewide service honestly. A local business should not claim offices in cities where it has none.

Avoid copied location pages and repeated keyword lists. A page earns its place by answering a distinct question or explaining a distinct service, with facts the business can support.

## Make answers easy to understand

Answer the main question directly, then explain conditions and practical steps. Use headings that identify the subject and links with useful descriptions.

[Google’s AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says its existing SEO best practices remain relevant to AI search features. There is no special schema or file that guarantees inclusion in an AI answer.

## Separate launch from ongoing work

New articles, review processes, Business Profile upkeep, analytics review, and earning relevant mentions continue after launch. Define who does that work and how results are assessed.

Use [Google’s SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) as a baseline. Ask your provider to explain the implementation in ordinary language.

## Put search requirements into acceptance

Ask for a representative live page with its intended title, canonical URL, indexability, useful internal links, and complete service text. Verify the sitemap lists production URLs and Search Console belongs to the business. For a redesign, include the important old-to-new URL map. These are checkable tasks. A proposal that says only SEO-ready should explain which of them it includes and who handles content improvements afterward.

## Before you decide

- Name technical deliverables.
- Review service content.
- Confirm Search Console access.
- Separate ongoing work from launch setup.

## Does a custom website rank better automatically?

No. Useful content, technical quality, relevant authority, and competition matter more than the label custom.
