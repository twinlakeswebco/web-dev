A simple, honest process

Five steps, start to finish, with no stretch of silence in the middle.

  1. Tell me about your business

    We start with a conversation about what you do, who you serve, and what your website needs to accomplish.

  2. We choose a practical plan

    I recommend an approach that fits your goals and your budget, and I explain what each option means before you commit.

  3. I design and build the site

    You'll see progress along the way instead of silence until a big reveal at the end.

  4. You review it and we refine

    You tell me what's right and what isn't, and we work through the details together.

  5. I launch it and show you what comes next

    You'll know how to update your site, what it costs to keep running, and who to call when something needs attention.
