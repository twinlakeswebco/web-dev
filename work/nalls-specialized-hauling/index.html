Case study

Nall's Specialized Hauling

A complete redesign for an established hauling company, pairing its new logo and original drone photography with a faster, mobile-first website.

The business

Nall's Specialized Hauling moves equipment and oversized loads for contractors, farms, and property owners. Most of the people who need that service are not browsing. They have a piece of equipment sitting somewhere it can't stay, and they want to know two things: can you move it, and how fast can you get here.

What they needed

Before I built the current site, Nall's had an HTML website created in the mid-2010s. It was a decent site for its time and had helped the company build valuable search visibility, but its design, content, and presentation had become severely outdated. The company requested a complete redesign that would preserve that SEO foundation while presenting the scale and professionalism of the business more accurately. The project also needed to introduce Nall's new logo and make room for original drone shots of its trucks, trailers, and operations.

What I built

I redesigned the website from the ground up around the company's new logo, using a stronger visual system and original drone photography to show the fleet and the work instead of relying on generic stock images. I reorganized the pages and content so visitors and search engines could still find the important service information without carrying the dated layout forward.

The new site is built for phones first, because many customers visit it from a job site or while arranging a load. Services are described in straightforward language, and the contact path stays short and visible. The static build also loads quickly on a weak signal and keeps the site simple to maintain.

What mattered most

  • Speed on mobile data, since customers are rarely on wifi when they need a hauler.
  • Plain descriptions of what does and does not get hauled, to filter out calls that waste both sides' time.
  • Clear service-area language so customers understand where Nall's operates.
  • Original drone photography and the new company logo, giving the redesign a visual identity that belongs to the business.
  • Preserving useful content and search signals from the previous site while replacing its outdated presentation.
  • Business information registered in the owner's name, so the site and domain stay theirs.
