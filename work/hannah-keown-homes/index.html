Hannah Keown Homes

A real estate website that helps buyers explore properties, gives sellers a place to start, and makes it easy to reach Hannah.

The business

Hannah Keown helps Kentucky buyers and sellers navigate their next move. Her website brings her real estate services, property search, and practical resources together under her own brand.

What she needed

A prospective client may arrive ready to look at homes, ask about selling, or simply learn how the process works. The site needed to support each of those starting points and give visitors a clear way to contact Hannah.

What I built

A custom real estate website with MLS property search, including address search, and dedicated information for buyers and sellers. Visitors can explore homes and learn about Hannah's services without having to piece everything together across separate sites.

Downloadable buyer, seller, and combined guides give visitors resources they can keep. An email step before downloading creates a contact opportunity for Hannah, while contact links throughout the site give people a direct next step when they are ready to talk.

What mattered most

  • Property search that serves visitors who are actively looking for a home.
  • Separate starting points for buyers and sellers.
  • Useful guides connected to a clear inquiry path.
  • A consistent personal brand across the website.
