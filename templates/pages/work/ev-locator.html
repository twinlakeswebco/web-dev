EV Locator

A community resource directory built to help people in Grayson County, Kentucky, find local services.

What the project needed

Someone looking for a food pantry, help with rent, or a ride to a medical appointment may know what they need without knowing which organization to call. EV Locator needed a single place for people in Grayson County to start looking.

What I built

I built a free, searchable directory with categories for food, housing, health care, transportation, job training, legal aid, family support, and financial help. Visitors can pick a category from the homepage or search the directory by what they need or where they live.

How people use it

A visitor can select Food & Meals or search for a term like “rent” to find relevant organizations. They can browse without an account, find contact details for providers, or request help through EV Locator if they need guidance.
