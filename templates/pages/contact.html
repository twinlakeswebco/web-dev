What Is a Revision Round?

A revision round is one collected list of feedback on your website preview. For example, you might ask us to adjust colors, replace photos, update wording, and rearrange a section. We make those changes together, then send you an updated preview. New pages, new features, or a different project direction may require an updated quote.

What About Ongoing Costs?

Hosting setup means connecting and configuring the service that makes your website available online. Domain registration, renewals, paid hosting, business email, software subscriptions, and transaction fees are separate unless your proposal specifically includes them.

Your proposal will also explain who supplies the text, photos, and branding, along with any support included after launch.
